# Crop Requirements Overview

## Lettuce
A fast-growing leafy green and one of the most forgiving hydroponic crops
for beginners. It prefers pH 5.5-6.5, moderate EC (0.8-1.8 mS/cm), cool air
temperature (18-24°C), and 14-16 hours of moderate-intensity light. It
grows best in NFT or DWC systems and is harvested before true flowering
(bolting) — bolted leaves turn bitter.

## Basil
An aromatic, high-value herb suited to NFT, DWC, Drip, or Vertical Farming
systems. It prefers warmer conditions than lettuce (20-27°C air
temperature), pH 5.5-6.5, and EC 1.0-1.6 mS/cm. Basil is sensitive to cold
water, which can shock its roots, and benefits from pinching growing tips
to encourage bushy growth and higher yield.

## Tomato
A high-value fruiting crop requiring strong trellis support, higher EC
(2.0-3.5 mS/cm depending on stage), and consistent pollination management
(often by gently vibrating trusses in an indoor setup). It suits Drip, DWC,
or Ebb and Flow systems, and its EC and light requirements increase
significantly once it enters the flowering/fruiting stage.

## Cucumber
A vigorous vining crop with high water demand, well suited to Drip, NFT,
DWC, or Ebb and Flow systems with vertical trellising. It prefers pH
5.5-6.0, humidity 60-80%, and consistent water delivery — irregular supply
during fruiting causes bitter or misshapen fruit.

## Hydroponic Barley Fodder
A fast-cycle livestock fodder crop grown as a dense sprout mat, with a very
short 7-9 day growth cycle and low light requirements compared to other
crops. It starts as a dark germination phase requiring only plain water,
then moves to a light nutrient solution and low light as shoots green up,
and is harvested — root mat and all — once the mat is dense and shoots
reach 15-20cm.
